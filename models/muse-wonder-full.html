Muse Wonder Full

Every published VulcanBench measurement of Muse Wonder Full (Meta), newest first: combined score, Code quality, runtime, tokens and an estimated cost at every effort level Muse Code offers in Contributor mode on Frontier v4, graded by deterministic hidden tests on real tasks. Measured through Muse Code 1.4.6 and 1.4.9 on a Muse Code subscription in Contributor mode, where Meta may train on submitted content, with Muse Code’s skill reminder off. Its Code quality is judged by Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol, a different panel from the other columns, because Muse Spark 1.3 is a Meta model. For the earlier Muse Spark 1.2, see its own page.

Muse Wonder Full across every effort level
In Muse Code across four effort levels: combined score 86.95 at Low, 88.27 at Medium, 88.19 at High and 87.59 at Extra-high (judged n=23, 23, 22, 22; 84.36 and 83.78 at High and Extra-high with one 3-hour timeout each counted as 0). Tasks passed 18, 21, 19 and 19 of 23; 27.3 to 36.2 minutes and an estimated $3.93 to $5.08 a task. Best level Medium, 23rd of 67 columns on the board.
