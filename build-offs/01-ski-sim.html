Build-off No. 01

Ski simulators: Chamonix Ski Sim and Tahoe Ski Adventures

Two browser ski games, each built by a model. Play both, then vote for the one you prefer.
2026-11-22 · Morgan Linton · browser games · 2 entries · reader vote

A build-off takes an open-ended brief, lets each model build a complete working thing, and puts the results side by side for you to try. This is the first one: two skiing games that run in a browser. VulcanBench does not score either one. Play both, then vote for the one you would rather have shipped.

Chamonix Ski Sim

Google · Gemini · Google AI Studio

A free-ski simulator set on the Vallée Blanche in the Mont Blanc massif. Its own controls list a helmet view, a time-of-day setting, a scout view, replays, avalanches and switch riding.

Play Chamonix Ski Sim
Opens in Google AI Studio in a new tab. It may ask you to sign in with a Google account.

Tahoe Ski Adventures

VulcanBench · Claude Fable 5.2 · Claude Code

A ski game on an invented, procedurally generated mountain that looks down on Lake Tahoe, with place names borrowed from the resorts around it. A 43-gate race with medal times and a ghost of your best run, a free-ride mode with other skiers, tricks and avalanches, and four weather settings.

Play Tahoe Ski Adventures
Opens on this site in a new tab. No sign-in. Your best times are kept in your browser only.

Both games show their controls on screen and both are built for a keyboard or a touch screen. Give each a few runs before you make up your mind.

What to look for

There is no rubric. These are the questions I would ask of both:

  • The first minute. Can you work out how to start and steer without reading anything?
  • The skiing. Does carving, speed and falling feel like something you can learn and get better at?
  • The mountain. How does it look and sound, and does it hold up when you stop and look around?
  • The second run. Is there a reason to go again?
  • The rough edges. What broke, stuttered or confused you, and on what device?

Why this is not a benchmark

  • The conditions differ. Chamonix Ski Sim is a published showcase. Its brief, the number of attempts behind it and how much a person edited it are not public. Tahoe Ski Adventures was built afterwards, by a model that had seen it and was asked for something similar, and it has had two further rounds of graphics work since it was first published here, one of them prompted by a fault I found while playing it.
  • They are not the same game. Tahoe Ski Adventures is not a copy. Despite its name, its Tahoe place names and the lake in its view, its mountain is invented, where Google’s is set on a real one, and it adds a gate race that Google’s does not have. The features do not line up one to one.
  • One sample each. A single build from each side, with no repeat runs, no fixed task and no rubric, says little about what either model does on average.
  • The vote is a show of hands. Voters choose themselves, they know which model built which game, and nothing checks that they played both. One vote per network address is a courtesy limit that is easy to get around. The tally says which game this page’s readers preferred, and nothing more general about either model.
  • VulcanBench is not neutral here. I commissioned one of the two entries, and Claude Fable 5.2, the model that built it, also drafted this page. I have kept the page to facts and to links so that the judging stays with you.

A controlled version would give every model the same brief, the same tools and the same time, with no sight of the other entries, and then collect votes on unlabelled builds. This build-off does none of that. Treat it as two demos side by side.

Notes

Tahoe Ski Adventures on this site. The playable build is served from this site and is the build Fable 5.2 wrote, with six later changes, each made by Fable 5.2 at my request. Nine long dashes in its on-screen text were replaced with colons and hyphens to meet this site’s writing rule. It was renamed: Fable 5.2 called it Fall Line, and it was published here under that name on November 22, 2026, before I renamed it Tahoe Ski Adventures later that day. And its place names were re-themed: Fable 5.2 had set it on a fictional French peak, and the sections of the run, the lifts and the signs now carry Lake Tahoe names. Along the way, board lettering that read mirrored on one side of the piste was fixed. After that came two changes that go beyond naming. Its graphics were reworked on November 22, 2026: the far peaks and the sea of cloud below them gave way to a view of the lake and the ranges beyond it, the valley walls were reshaded as granite and snow, and the trees were remodelled. And it now opens in feet and miles per hour, where it used to open in metres and kilometres per hour; the other units are still on the title screen. Last, the skier was remodelled the same day, after I played the game and saw its legs come apart from its boots in turns: the legs now follow the boots, and the figure, boots, bindings, skis and poles were rebuilt. The course, the physics and the medal times did not change, and votes cast under the old name carry over. Any vote cast before these reworks was for the game as it looked then. It bundles three.js under the MIT License; the notice is in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt.

The resort names. Sections and lifts in Tahoe Ski Adventures are named for four Lake Tahoe ski resorts: Northstar, Diamond Peak, Mt. Rose and Kirkwood. The names are a nod to those places and nothing more. The game is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of those resorts, and its terrain does not model theirs.

The vote. A vote stores your choice, the time, and a keyed hash of your network address that is used only to keep it to one vote per address. The address itself is not stored and the hash cannot be turned back into it without a key that stays on the server. No account and no cookie are involved; your browser remembers your choice so the page can show it again.

Chamonix Ski Sim. It is Google’s app and Google hosts it, so it may change or move after this page was written. The description above is from its app page as it stood on November 22, 2026.
