What a build-off is

A showcase comparison, not a benchmark.

01

An open-ended brief

Each entry is a whole product built by a model from a short request: a game, a tool, an app. There is no hidden test and no single right answer.

02

You are the judge

Every entry is linked so you can use it yourself, then vote for the one you prefer. VulcanBench does not score or rank the entries, and the tally is a reader poll, not a measurement.

03

Conditions disclosed

Each page says how every entry was made, as far as that is known: model, tool, brief, human involvement and testing. Unknowns are marked as unknown.
