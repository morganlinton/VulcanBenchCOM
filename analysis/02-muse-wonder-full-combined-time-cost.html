Muse Wonder Full against Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra: combined score, time and cost

2026-10-15 · Morgan Linton · VulcanBench Frontier v4, Low and Medium effort · combined score, time and cost

With its code now judged, Muse Wonder Full scores 87.0 on the combined score at Low and 88.8 at Medium. At Low that is level with Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra; at Medium it is second, behind Opus 5.5. It is also the slowest and most expensive of the four, at about 27 minutes and about $3.90 per task.

Combined score

  • Low: a four-way tie. All four models are within 1.2 points (86.2 to 87.4), and the standard errors are 0.5 to 1.3 points, so no order at Low is reliable. Muse Wonder Full's 87.0 sits between Astra's 87.4 and Opus 5.5's 86.4.
  • Medium: second. Muse Wonder Full's 88.8 is behind Opus 5.5's 90.9 and ahead of Astra (87.7) and GPT-6.1 Sol (86.4). This level is preliminary until its last two tasks run.
  • Functional score. Muse Wonder Full passed 95.8% of hidden tests at Low (18 of 23 tasks fully solved) and 99.0% at Medium (20 of 21).

Time and cost

  • Time. At Low, Muse Wonder Full takes 27.3 minutes per task: about 2x Opus 5.5, 4x GPT-6.1 Sol and 6.5x Astra. At Medium the gap narrows against Opus 5.5 (25.6 against 18.6 minutes) but not against Astra (3.8). Analysis No. 01 traces where that time goes: about 3x more model calls and 10x more output than GPT-6.1 Sol, not slower steps.
  • Cost. About $3.93 per task at Low and $3.90 at Medium: about 2.3x Opus 5.5 and Astra at Low, and about 10x to 13x GPT-6.1 Sol. Meta lists no API price for Muse Wonder Full, so this is an estimate from its token use at the Muse Wonder 1.0 list price ($4.00 input, $0.20 cached, $20.00 output per million tokens).
  • Value. At Low, GPT-6.1 Sol reaches about the same combined score for about a twelfth of the cost and a quarter of the time. At Medium, Muse Wonder Full's extra 2.4 points over Sol cost about ten times as much per task.

How the code was judged

Code quality is a third of the combined score, and two calibrated model judges score it. Each judge first passes an exam on held-out code samples under the same frozen protocol. The other three models were judged by Muse Spark 1.3 and Grok 4.6. Muse Spark is a Meta model, so it is not neutral for a Meta model, and Muse Wonder Full was judged by Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol instead. Both passed the exam with no failing gate.

  • Different panels. Scores from different judge panels are not calibrated against each other, so small gaps between Muse Wonder Full and the others should be read with care.
  • The two judges disagreed. Reviewed Code quality was 59 from Opus 5.5 and 76 from GPT-6.1 Sol at Low, and 60 and 76 at Medium, out of 100. The combined score uses their mean. Scored by one judge alone, Muse Wonder Full's combined score would be 85.0 (Opus 5.5) or 88.9 (GPT-6.1 Sol) at Low, and 86.9 or 90.7 at Medium.
  • A judge that is also compared. Claude Opus 5.5 judged Muse Wonder Full and also appears in this comparison. Its own combined score comes from its own round, judged by Muse Spark 1.3 and Grok 4.6.

Method and caveats

Runs. Muse Wonder Full (muse-wonder-full-1.0, Contributor mode) in Muse Code 1.4.6 with its skill reminder off, at Low (23 tasks, 13 to 14 October 2026) and Medium (21 of 23 tasks, 14 to 15 October 2026). The last two Medium tasks run after the Muse Code weekly limit resets on 19 October; this page will be updated when they and their judging are done. The other models are their published Frontier v4 runs: Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code 2.1.280, GPT-6.1 Sol in Codex 0.159.0 and GPT-6 Astra in Codex.

Judging. Muse Wonder Full's Code quality is protocol v3.26 (Claude Opus 5.5 at medium effort) with GPT-6.1 Sol's v3.25 scores (medium effort) on byte-identical inputs. Opus 5.5 is v3.15, GPT-6.1 Sol v3.18 and GPT-6 Astra v3.4.

Cost. List prices were checked on 15 October 2026. Opus 5.5 is Claude Code's own total, which reprices exactly from its traces. GPT-6.1 Sol is its Codex token receipts at $2.00 / $0.10 / $10.00 per million. GPT-6 Astra ($10 / $1 / $50) is its report's central estimate; it and GPT-6.1 Sol charge more on requests over 272K input tokens, and request sizes were not recorded, so + marks Astra's upper bound ($3.28 at Low, $2.79 at Medium). GPT-6.1 Sol's calls average about 35K input tokens, so its premium is likely small.

Not controlled. The harnesses differ, and minutes are wall-clock time in each model's own harness. Seven of Muse Wonder Full's finished Medium tasks overlapped another local job, which can lengthen minutes but does not change scores. One run per task and level.
