Notes on the board and on individual columns

17 models, 80 model×effort columns, 1,808 runs. Combined score is 50% functional correctness, 8.5% lint and complexity, 8.5% security and 33% Code quality, judged for a human reader by Muse Spark 1.3 and Grok 4.6 under one frozen protocol (v3.4 to v3.7, v3.15 to v3.18, v3.20, v3.23, v3.24, v3.26, v3.27, v3.28 and v3.29 apply the same rubric, controls and gates to each population; v3.20 seats GPT-6.1 Sol in place of Grok 4.6 to judge Grok 4.7, see ◊; v3.23 and v3.24 judge Grok 4.6 through a newer Cursor CLI, see †† and ‡‡; v3.26 to v3.26.3 seat Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol to judge Muse Wonder Full, see §§; v3.27 and v3.28 seat GPT-6.1 Sol in place of Grok 4.6 to judge DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Reflection Beam, see ¶¶ and ◊◊). The shaded top-left corner of the chart is the quadrant to focus on: levels that score 80 or more for less than the median cost (or tokens, or minutes) of the plotted levels. Completion tokens are the model's own output per task, reasoning included. $/task is API-equivalent at list rates from the solver receipts; every model here ran on a subscription except DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, which ran on a metered DeepSeek API key and is shown at DeepSeek’s off-peak list price, see ¶¶. Grok 4.7 and Reflection Beam have no list price, so their $/task is unavailable and they appear on the Tokens and Minutes views only. Muse Wonder Full’s $/task is an estimate at the Muse Wonder 1.0 list price, see §§.
