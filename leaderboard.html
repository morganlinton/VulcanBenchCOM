Between suites

The board is paused.

A new eval suite is being built, so the combined leaderboard is offline rather than ranking models on a suite that is on its way out.

Eval Suite 3 is frozen. Its measurements are not going anywhere: every model that ran on it, at every reasoning-effort level, is documented in the published reports, and the underlying run data stays in the repository. What is gone for now is the single combined table, because a board is only worth reading when the suite behind it is the one being measured.

Ranking on a saturated suite is the failure mode this project exists to avoid. Twelve of the fifteen models on the final Eval Suite 3 board sat within one standard error of the leader, which is a polite way of saying the order at the top was not supported by the data. The new suite gates tasks on measured difficulty before they are admitted, and the board comes back once enough columns have run on it to rank anything honestly.

In the meantime, the reports carry the per-task detail, cost, speed, and effort curves for every measurement, and the methodology page describes how the new suite is built and gated.
